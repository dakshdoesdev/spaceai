| Queue reset -> generated artifacts | Reset operation deletes files and must stay scoped to generated queue artifacts. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-01-01-02 | Denial of Service | `--reset-queue` | mitigate | Do not recurse into arbitrary directories except the runner-owned `crops/` directory. |
| T-01-01-03 | Repudiation | `simulate_orbital_pass` | accept | Local CLI has no authenticated actor model; freshness is proven by queue contents and telemetry tests. |
</threat_model>
